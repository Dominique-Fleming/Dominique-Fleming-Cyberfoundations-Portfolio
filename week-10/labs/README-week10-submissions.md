# Week 10 Submissions Guide

This guide lives in `week-10/labs/`. It explains how to move your two Demo Lab reports into your own GitHub portfolio and record them in the Lab Submission Tracker. For the week overview, see the [Week 10 root README](../README-week10-root.md).

## The Two Required Reports

| Lab | Button in the Demo Lab | Save it in your repository as |
|---|---|---|
| Lab 1 — Investigate What Needs Protection | **Download Lab 1 report (Markdown)** | `week-10/labs/week10-lab-1.md` |
| Lab 2 — Prioritize Risks and Recommend Controls | **Download Lab 2 report (Markdown)** | `week-10/labs/week10-lab-2.md` |

Only these two `.md` reports count as your Week 10 lab submissions. The **JSON backup** and the optional **combined portfolio report** are not substitutes for them.

## Before You Download: Checklists

These checks confirm your work is present. They are not grading — your instructor reviews the quality of your reasoning.

**Lab 1**
- [ ] I visited all five rooms: Reception, Records Office, Staff Workspace, Website Station and Backup Room.
- [ ] I collected at least five findings with **Add to my findings**. (Collecting a finding does not attach it to a scenario.)
- [ ] I completed all five scenarios, SC-01 to SC-05, covering all five assets.
- [ ] Each scenario has evidence I selected in **Evidence I am using**, plus Threat / event, Vulnerability, Consequence and CIA concern(s). Unknown / question is optional.
- [ ] I wrote three warning signs, a safe response and reporting step, and my suspicious-vs-proven reflection.

**Lab 2**
- [ ] All five scenarios have a Likelihood (1–3) and Impact (1–3), each with its reason.
- [ ] I chose two priorities and explained why.
- [ ] Each priority has a Control, How it helps and Risk remaining afterwards.
- [ ] I wrote a manager briefing (about 100–150 words is a flexible guide).
- [ ] I kept my Lab 1 work in place — I did not reset it.

## Step by Step: Upload a Report to GitHub

1. In the Demo Lab, enter or check your name, then select the download button for the lab.
2. Open **your own** CyberFoundations portfolio repository on GitHub — not the institute's template repository.
3. Open the `week-10` folder, then the `labs` folder.
4. Select **Add file > Upload files** and choose the downloaded report (`week10-lab-1.md` or `week10-lab-2.md`).
5. Check the filename and that the destination is `week-10/labs/`.
6. Enter a meaningful commit message, such as `Add Week 10 Lab 1 report`, and select **Commit changes**. Committing means saving the file in your repository.
7. Open the committed file on GitHub and confirm your name and latest answers display.
8. In the Lab Submission Tracker (https://labsubmission.cybervisionariesinstitute.org), use the existing **Submit** for that lab. Do this once for Lab 1 and once for Lab 2.

Downloading a report does not upload it. Uploading to GitHub does not record it in the tracker. Nothing syncs automatically.

## If `week-10/labs/` Does Not Exist Yet

If you already saved this guide or another file into `week-10/labs/`, the folder exists — just use it. Only if the folder is missing:

1. From your repository's main page, select **Add file > Create new file**.
2. Type `week-10/labs/README.md` as the name. Each `/` creates a folder.
3. Add a short heading such as `# Week 10 Lab Reports` and select **Commit changes**.
4. Open `week-10/labs` and upload your report as above.

Never replace an existing `README.md`. That small helper file only creates the folder; it is different from this guide, `README-week10-submissions.md`.

## Revising a Report

1. Update your answers in the Demo Lab and download a fresh report.
2. If your browser renamed it (for example `week10-lab-1 (1).md`), rename it back to the original name.
3. Upload it to the same `week-10/labs/` folder, confirm GitHub shows you are updating the intended file, then commit.
4. Reopen the file and check your latest answers.
5. Follow the tracker's existing resubmission process for that lab, as shown in the tracker or by your instructor.

Do not delete other files or folders in your repository.

## Supporting Files (Notes and Reflection)

Your Week 10 [notes](../notes.md) and [reflection](../reflection.md) are completed in the Lab Portal. Use **Save Progress** there, and the portal's existing **Submit to GitHub** option to place them at `week-10/notes.md` and `week-10/reflection.md` in your portfolio. They are supporting work: they do not replace or count as the Lab 1 or Lab 2 tracker submissions.

## Keeping Demo Work Safe

- The Demo Lab saves in this browser on this device only. Wait for the **Saved** indicator.
- Download the JSON backup before switching devices or clearing browser data.
- **Restore from backup file** replaces your current demo work after you confirm.
- Screenshots are optional.

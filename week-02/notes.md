## In My Own Words

**What is the cybersecurity landscape, and why does it matter?**

```
Cybersecurity landscape is the four moving parts (attackers, defenders, infrastructure, everyday users) are like the "circle of life" for the IT world. Its important to know that they all have a place and purpose because without 1 the others may not have a job, improvements/advancements etc. We need to also understand each so that we can know foundations and the motivations behind the constantly adapting and every changing environment.
```

**How do hardware, OS, and software layers interact?**

```
Hardware the bottom layer is like the tank of the 3 layers because it holds a lot of responsibility supporting the demands of the other 2 layers(do we have the capacity, do we have the room, do we have the brain power), the OS is like the Strategist because it needs to organize, plan, place and assign purpose to all the communication between the Hardware and the Software. And the top layer has Software which is like the Front man. They are the ones that get all the initial information, task, request etc from users(everyday user, attackers, defenders etc) and then have to oblige by taking all of those things in at once smoothly and seeing how quick they can dumb down the information to make sure the other 2 layers can understand the needs and negotiate on what they can get from them.
```

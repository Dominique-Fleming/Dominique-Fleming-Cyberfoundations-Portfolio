> ### 🔒 Cloud Heights Security Rule
> Your Bastion link and Cloud Heights password are **private access credentials**. Never paste either into a worksheet, screenshot, GitHub repository, Circle post, or chat message. When taking screenshots, crop out the browser address bar and all login information.

---

## What Week 6 Asks of You

Week 6 moves you from a simulator to a **live Ubuntu 22.04 machine in Azure**. Your Cloud Heights room is already reserved for you — you never create or pay for it. You start it, work in it, and it shuts itself down after 15 minutes of inactivity (you get a warning and a **Keep Working** button first).

**There is no flagship deliverable this week.** Instead, all five core labs commit to your portfolio repository, and together they become your Week 6 evidence.

## The Labs

| Lab | Title | Style | Time | Graded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Claiming Your Room in Cloud Heights | Guided | 30–40 min | Core — graded |

| 05 | Layer Detective | Independent — **SHORT**, no VM | 20–30 min | Core — graded |

**Labs 04 and 05 are intentionally short.** They are small on purpose, not incomplete.

**The stretch lab is genuinely optional.** Skipping it costs nothing and does not make your submission lesser.

## How to Submit

1. Sign in to the CyberFoundations Lab Portal.
2. Open **Week 6** and click into a lab.
3. Fill in the worksheet fields — your answers save as you work.
4. Connect GitHub once and select your portfolio repository.
5. Upload that lab's screenshots to `assets/screenshots/week-06/` in your repo.
6. Click **Submit to GitHub**.
